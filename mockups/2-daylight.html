Questions students actually ask

My school isn't in there. Now what?

Add it. Start from a template, enter the bell schedule once, and it's there for everyone after you. At ten members it locks so it can't be quietly broken.

Who can see my schedule?

Only the people you share it with. Sharing is per‑person and you can undo it at any time.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It's a website you can add to your home screen. No Apple account, no Play Store, no updates to install.

What if my schedule is different from everyone else's?

Shift times, close a date, add a one‑off period, or run an entirely private schedule. School corrections never overwrite your personal changes.

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no ads, no selling anything about you.
