T
Tuesday · Snow day

School is closed. So is the rotation.

This is where most apps quietly break: they keep counting. Tomorrow they'll say Day 7 when the school says Day 6, and they'll be wrong every day for the rest of the month. Quasar treats a closure as a schedule concept — the cycle holds, and every date after it is re‑derived.

❄Cycle paused · Day 6 moves to Wednesday
W
Wednesday · Two‑hour delay

Same classes. Every single time is wrong.

The order of the day is right, but nothing starts when the bell schedule says. One control shifts the whole day — as your own adjustment or as the school's — and every start, end, countdown and reminder moves with it.

⏱Whole day shifted +2h · countdown follows
T
Thursday · Runs as a Monday

“Thursday will run on a Monday schedule.”

Different periods, different order, same date — and in most apps, no way to say it without editing every class by hand and remembering to undo it. In Quasar it's one date pointed at one cycle day. The rotation underneath keeps counting normally.

⇄Replacement day · your overrides still apply
F
Friday · Basement, no signal

You add a lab report. The Wi‑Fi is gone.

It saves on your device immediately and uploads when you're back — and the status pill always tells you where it stands. If another device edited the same thing, you get both values and pick. Nothing is silently overwritten and nothing is lost to a bad connection.
