§3 Non-goals

Things Quasar will not do
  • ✕Advertising. No ad slots, no “sponsored” rows in your timetable.
  • ✕Tracking. No analytics pixels, no fingerprinting, no third-party SDKs.
  • ✕Selling data. Nothing about you is a product line.
  • ✕Silent overwrites. The app never picks a winner for you.
  • ✕Blank screens. No signal is a normal case, not an error state.
  • ✓Sharing is on your terms. Send your schedule to the friends you choose, one at a time, and take it back whenever. (planned)
