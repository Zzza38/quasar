Straight answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid tier, no ads, no selling data. It's a personal project, not a startup runway.

Who can see my schedule?

Only the people you choose. Sharing is per‑person, off until you turn it on, and you can take it back at any time.

What if my school isn't listed?

Add it in a few minutes. Everyone after you gets it for free, and once ten people join it locks so it can't be quietly changed.

What happens to my edits when I'm offline?

They save on your device and upload when you're back. If another device changed the same thing, you get both values and pick — never a silent overwrite.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It's a website. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app, including offline.
